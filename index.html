Welcome to the Department of Information Technology

The Department of Information Technology provides students with knowledge and practical skills in computing, software development, networking, databases, web development and other areas of information technology.

The department prepares students to use technology to solve real-world problems and contribute to the modern digital economy.

Our IT Laboratory

Our laboratory provides students with a practical environment where they can develop computer and technology skills.

Information Technology Courses

The department offers a range of courses designed to develop students' technical and problem-solving abilities.

  • Web Development
  • Database Management Systems
  • Computer Networks
  • Software Engineering
  • Programming
  • Information Systems
